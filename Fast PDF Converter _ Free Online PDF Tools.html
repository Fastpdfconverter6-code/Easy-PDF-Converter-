      Questions

      Are my files uploaded?

      No. Every tool works inside your browser with your device's own memory. Nothing is sent to a server, and closing the tab clears your files.

      Is there a size limit?

      There is no fixed limit. Very large PDFs depend on your phone or computer's memory, so a 200 MB file may be slow on an older phone.

      Why can't I select text after compressing?

      Compress turns each page into an optimized image to shrink the file. That is why it works on scanned and image-heavy PDFs, but the text is no longer selectable.

      Can I add or remove a password?

      Not yet. Password-protected PDFs are detected and reported, so remove the password first with the app that created the file.
